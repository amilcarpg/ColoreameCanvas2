Website and app privacy: technical draft

This draft describes the current implementation. The operator, contact information, applicable markets and complete legal notice remain to be supplied before activating optional services.

Your drawings

The website stores drawings in this browser on this device, using IndexedDB with a localStorage fallback. The app stores drawings locally. Saving can fail if storage is unavailable or full. Browser storage and operating system backups depend on your device settings.

Downloading, printing or sharing can send a copy to your browser, printer or another application you choose. Bucket and brush maintain separate local work.

Optional services

External analytics and advertising are disabled in the supplied configuration. Google Analytics for the website and Firebase Analytics for the mobile app are prepared as optional integrations. They require valid project configuration and a review of the applicable treatment before activation. A preference button or an adult interface does not verify parental consent.

If analytics is activated later, Google SDKs can process device and usage information beyond the application's custom event fields. Their use must be described in a completed notice. You can paint without granting optional permissions.

Control local storage

Download any drawing you want to keep before clearing browser or app storage. Local copies may be lost after clearing storage, uninstalling or ending a private browsing session.
