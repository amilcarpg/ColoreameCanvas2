Dinosaur coloring together

Pick a drawing and explore colors at your own pace. No account is needed. This free activity includes four dinosaurs and tools for coloring, saving and printing.

Prepare the activity with an adult

  1. Choose a dinosaur together. Start with the bucket to fill a region, or select the brush in the adult area.
  2. Let the child choose two or three colors. Try a large area first, then explore the details.
  3. Use Undo and Redo to compare choices. There is no required result or time limit.
  4. Check the save status before leaving. A local copy belongs to this browser, device and tool; switching tools keeps separate work.

Save or continue on paper

Download PNG to keep a separate image. Open the downloaded file to check it. To color on paper, open an uncolored drawing and choose Print drawing in the adult area. Check paper size, orientation and margins in the browser preview.

Clearing browser storage or using another device can remove or hide your local copy. Browser private sessions may not retain it. Painting works with optional analytics rejected.
